A practical place to learn, practice and improve

MeritArc brings subject practice and exam preparation into one simple workspace. You can start with a focused technical subject such as Windows, Linux, VMware or Azure, build core computer science skills, or move into preparation for competitive examinations. The goal is to make each practice session useful: choose a topic, attempt a fresh set of questions, review the explanations and use your result to decide what to study next.

Practice by subject

Subject based practice is useful when you already know where you need improvement. MeritArc currently organizes technical infrastructure, computer science and aptitude subjects separately so you can work on one skill at a time instead of opening a large mixed question bank.

Explore exam preparation

The Exam Library brings banking, SSC, teaching, railway, government and defence preparation pages together with the existing technical and academic practice areas. Each exam page explains the main preparation areas and links to the relevant MeritArc practice subjects.

Use practice as a feedback loop

A useful practice routine is more than collecting scores. After a session, review the questions you missed, identify whether the problem was knowledge, calculation, interpretation or time management, and then return to that topic. Repeating this cycle makes your preparation more deliberate.

What you can find on MeritArc

MeritArc includes original practice questions, explanations, assessment history for signed in users and preparation guidance for a growing set of exams. Exam specific information is presented as a study aid, while official examination authorities remain the source for current eligibility rules, dates, vacancies, syllabi and selection requirements. Previous year questions are kept distinct from original practice material and are only described as previous year material when they have been verified and identified appropriately.
