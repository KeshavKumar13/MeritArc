How to prepare effectively

Use short daily sessions for mathematics and reasoning, then rotate general science and general awareness topics. Track accuracy separately from speed: a fast method that produces repeated errors needs correction before it becomes a habit. Mixed practice near the end of each week helps build exam stamina.

1. Build accuracy first

Start with untimed or lightly timed topic sets until the method is reliable. Record the reason behind every mistake: concept gap, calculation error, interpretation error or avoidable selection mistake.

2. Introduce timed practice

Once accuracy is stable, use short timed sets. The goal is not simply to answer faster; it is to identify which question types consume disproportionate time and adjust the solving order.

3. Review instead of repeating blindly

A second attempt at the same type of question is most useful when the first attempt has been analyzed. Keep a small revision list of formulas, rules, vocabulary, concepts and question patterns that repeatedly cause difficulty.

Previous-year questions and original practice

Previous-year questions can help candidates understand recurring patterns and difficulty. MeritArc distinguishes original practice questions from questions that are explicitly identified as previous-year material. We do not label an original question as a previous-year question. When a question set is added as genuine previous-year material, its year and source will be identified on the relevant assessment.

Until then, candidates can use MeritArc's original practice sets to strengthen the same underlying skills without confusing practice material with an official paper.

Frequently asked questions

Does MeritArc replace the official notification?

No. MeritArc is a practice and preparation platform. Official exam bodies remain the authoritative source for eligibility, dates, syllabus, vacancies and selection rules.

Can I practice by subject?

Yes. Use the subject links on this page to open the relevant MeritArc assessment area. Your results can be tracked through the platform when you use an account.

Should I solve full mocks immediately?

Usually it is more useful to establish topic accuracy first and then increase the frequency of mixed timed practice. The right balance depends on your current preparation level.
