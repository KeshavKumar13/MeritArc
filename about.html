About MeritArc

MeritArc is a practice and assessment platform built to help learners organize preparation, solve questions and track progress across competitive exams, technical skills and professional subjects.

What MeritArc is designed to do

MeritArc brings practice questions, assessments, explanations and result tracking into one place. The platform is organized around the idea that useful preparation is a cycle: learn a concept, practice it, review mistakes and return to weak areas.

The platform covers technical and infrastructure subjects such as Windows, Linux, VMware, Hyper-V, Azure, AWS, security and networking, alongside computer science, aptitude and competitive examination preparation. The Exam Library adds exam-specific context so users can start with a target exam and then move into subject practice.

Our content approach

MeritArc aims to publish original explanations and practice questions. Questions created by MeritArc are not presented as official previous-year questions. When a resource is explicitly identified as previous-year material, the page will identify the relevant year and source information.

This distinction matters because a practice platform should help users learn without confusing original preparation material with an official examination paper.

Quality and improvement

Users can report questions that appear incorrect, unclear or incomplete. Reported issues can be reviewed and corrected through the MeritArc administration workflow. Assessment results are intended to help learners identify patterns in their preparation rather than act as a guarantee of examination performance.
