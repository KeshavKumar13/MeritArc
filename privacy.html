Privacy Policy

This page explains the information MeritArc may collect, how it is used, and the third-party services used to operate and measure the website.

Information you provide

If you create a MeritArc account, the platform may collect your name, email address and a securely stored password hash. Assessment activity associated with an account may include assessment attempts, answers, scores and related progress information. If you contact support or submit a question report, the information you provide may also be stored so the request can be handled.

Cookies and sessions

MeritArc uses a session cookie when an account is signed in so that the platform can recognize the authenticated session. The site may also use browser storage for guest assessment functionality. These technologies are used to operate account and assessment features.

Google Analytics

MeritArc uses Google Analytics 4 to understand website usage, such as page views and general interaction patterns. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. The information is used to understand site performance and improve the user experience.

Advertising and Google AdSense

MeritArc may use Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to provide, measure and personalize advertising where applicable. Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies on users' browsers in connection with advertising.

Users can review Google's information about how it uses data on partner sites and manage advertising preferences through Google's available controls.

How information is used

Information is used to provide account features, save assessment progress, respond to support requests, maintain platform security, understand site usage and improve MeritArc. MeritArc does not use account passwords in plain text; passwords are stored using a one-way password hashing process.

Data retention and security

MeritArc retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant account or service, comply with operational requirements and handle support or security issues. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to support@meritarc.in.

This page is general website privacy information and is not legal advice. Applicable privacy obligations can vary by jurisdiction and by the services enabled on the site.
